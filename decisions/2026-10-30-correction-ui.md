# Correcting struck text, resetting, and finding your way around

Struck-through text (`~~…~~`) can be deleted from the popup: an uncertain word offers "Delete struck text" beside its readings, and struck text that isn't uncertain is clickable for that alone. An uncertain word that is exactly a strike's inside (`tap` in `~~tap~~`) is widened to take in the tildes, alternatives too, on every parse, so no cache bump is needed. A wholly deleted word remembers where it was (shifted by later edits to its line, forgotten if the line is edited by hand), so its grey box can still put a reading back. Transcripts keep Claude's text from before the first correction: "Reset Transcript" (status row and Page menu, asking first) restores it, and unfound boxes say whether the word was edited out or was never in Claude's text. Clicking a box scrolls the transcript to its line, pointing at a line outlines its box on the page, and a single click on a line hints that a double-click edits it.
