# Clearing the transcription cache by hand, and switching it off

The app no longer prunes older `CACHE_VERSION` entries on startup. Bumps are rare, and a dev build and an installed build share the cache directory, so they mustn't delete each other's entries. Settings has a Transcript caching section instead. It shows the cache's location in a read-only text field (selectable, copyable, in the Tab order), a switch for using the cache (`Settings::cache_transcripts`, on by default), and a red Clear cache button with the entry count beside it, disabled while there are none. Clearing asks first, with a red Clear cache button, and happens once confirmed, whether or not Settings is then saved. Saving with caching newly switched off offers to delete what's cached, with a red Delete button and Don't delete in place of Cancel. While caching is off, pages are neither looked up nor stored, exactly as if there were no cache: without credentials the Transcribed view is disabled even though projects keep their transcripts. `notebook-tools --clear-caches-only` clears the cache and exits; it replaces the `clear-cache` binary, and `scripts/clear-cache.sh` runs it.

Reason: Human Taste (no pruning, the section and its wording, the two questions, the flag, keeping the location, off meaning no cache, the count and disabled button); unquestioned agent recommendation for the read-only text field and clearing immediately on confirm.
