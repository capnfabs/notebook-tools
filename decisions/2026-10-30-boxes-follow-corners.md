# Boxes follow the page's corners

Cache entries keep the corners the photo was cropped to when sent (the whole photo's if nothing was found), and transcripts carry them as a `Crop`. When the page is cropped differently now, its boxes are mapped onto the new crop by the perspective transform between the two (old crop → photo → new crop); that's exact for a flat page, so there's no cache-busting and no "Bounds changed" note. Uncertain boxes are drawn as the mapped quad, so a turned crop shows them slanted; the PDF's text layer uses their upright bounds (`2026-10-30-pdf-text-upright-boxes.md`). Two warnings remain: lines with over half their box newly outside the page ("N transcribed lines fall outside…"; what was already outside, as Claude's boxes overrun a little, doesn't count), and more than 10% of the page outside the crop Claude saw ("retranscribe to ensure you've got all text"). Entries stored before corners were were backfilled on load with the default detected corners, once; that's since been removed (`2026-10-30-cache-housekeeping.md`).

Reason: Human Taste (remap rather than bust, quiet small changes, the warnings and their wording, backfilling defaults); unquestioned agent recommendation for the line-based cut-off test, the thresholds and backfilling in the cache.
