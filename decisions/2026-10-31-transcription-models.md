# Opus or Sonnet, chosen in Settings

Settings picks Opus 5.5 (the default) or Sonnet 5.5, both at effort `high`, for new and forced transcriptions. Changing it changes nothing already loaded. Each model keeps its own cache entry per photo, and either will do for a page (`2026-10-31-transcription-cache.md`). Both are in the same high-resolution image tier (2576px, 4,784 visual tokens), so `MAX_IMAGE_PIXELS` holds for both. Eval loglines (target `eval`: tokens, cost, latency and session cost per model, comparisons with the other model's cached transcript, and each correction) are for judging the two.

On 5 journal pages corrected by hand, Sonnet was 2–3× faster (9–15s a page against 20–45s) and about $0.03 a page against $0.07, and read words about as well (98–99% the same text). But it flagged fewer uncertain words (16 against 27), so more mistakes went unflagged (10 against 7), its alternatives often repeat its reading, and it drops separators, underlines, superscripts and crossed-out words. So Sonnet suits a quick first pass, Opus pages you'll correct closely. Five pages is a small sample. Reason: Human Taste (keep both, cache key, lookup preference, logged events), from the agent's measurements.
