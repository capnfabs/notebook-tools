# Pages whose photos are missing are shown, as placeholders

A page whose photo is gone, changed or unreadable when its project opens stays in `Pages`, in its place, without its bytes (`Page::bytes` is `Err(Why)`), rather than being hidden and kept aside. The strip shows a tile with a warning sign; every view shows "Photo missing" and why where the image would go (once in the Cleaned view, without its split; in the Transcribed view, beside the transcript). Its saved transcript can be read, corrected and reset. It can't be transcribed, retranscribed or have its corners changed, and Transcribe All leaves it out. Deleting or moving it is an edit. The status line says once which photos are missing. A photo added with the same name as a missing page is skipped with an error, until pages stop being keyed by name (`tasks/page-ids.md`). Export refuses while a page in scope is missing, in every format for now (`tasks/missing-page-export.md`). The icon is drawn, since egui's bundled fonts have no ⚠.

Reason: Human Taste (summary line, tile, keeping both splits, greying out Force Retranscribe, the same-name error, interim export rule); unquestioned agent recommendation for keeping missing pages in `Pages` rather than beside it.
