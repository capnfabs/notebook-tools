# Manual page corners: in memory, no cache-busting

The Cleaned view is a split: the photo with the page's four corners on the left (the user's, else detected, else the photo's own), the page cleaned with them on the right. Corners can be dragged, or clicked in afresh with Place Corners; Reset Corners goes back to detection. The page is cleaned again from the full photo, in the background, when a corner is let go. Outlines must be page-shaped (`clean::is_page_shaped`: convex, every corner 15°–165°): past that, straightening maps part of the page towards infinity and `imageproc`'s warp panicked, aborting the app. `straighten` refuses other shapes; a dragged corner stops at the limit, and a fourth click that would spoil the shape is refused. The corners are per page, in memory only, and crop what's sent to Claude, but aren't in the cache key: a cached transcript stays until the user forces retranscription, and meanwhile its boxes follow the new corners (`2026-10-30-boxes-follow-corners.md`). This supersedes `superseded/2026-10-28-cache-key-ignores-clean-params.md`. The cleanup debug overlay is gone (see `tasks/live-clean-params.md`).

Reason: Human Taste (split view, no cache-busting, the note's wording, removing the overlay, constraining the shape); unquestioned agent recommendation for drag-plus-Place-Corners, cleaning on release, the 15° limit and keeping corners in memory (gap: `tasks/persist-corners.md`).
